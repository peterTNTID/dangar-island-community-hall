About Dangar Island Community Hall

Dangar Island is a picturesque, car-free river island situated in the magnificent Hawkesbury River (Dyarubbin) in New South Wales.

The Community Hall serves as the central gathering place for island residents, artists, musicians, fitness groups, and community meetings. Whether you are a lifelong resident, weekender, or river visitor, all are welcome.

⛴️ Getting Here: Hop on the Brooklyn ferry from Hawkesbury River Station.
📍 Location: A short stroll from the public ferry wharf along Riverview Ave.
♿ Facilities: Main hall, timber stage, kitchen facilities, accessible ramp, restrooms.
